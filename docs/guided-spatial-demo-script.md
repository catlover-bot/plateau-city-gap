# Guided spatial demo script

Goal: `guided-spatial-storytelling-v1`

This is a deterministic 30–60 second presentation path. It uses the real Maizuru catalog and the verified Tsune Area artifacts. It does not create a field observation or imply a municipal decision.

| Time | Presenter says | Action | Visible spatial response |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0–5 s | 「舞鶴の地域を、地図からたどります。」 | Select `デモを始める`. | The same map reveals the three shortlisted Areas and their names. |
| 5–15 s | 「一覧と地図は同じ候補を指しています。」 | Focus Nio, then focus/select Tsune. | The corresponding Area is emphasized; selection moves the camera to Tsune while the other candidates mute. |
| 15–30 s | 「公開データで気になった範囲を、PLATEAUの街の形まで見ます。」 | Select `街の形を見る`. | The same map zooms to the exact 500 m Area and draws 303 intersecting buildings, 135 road surfaces and eight planning shapes from the pinned Maizuru 2025 source. The verified A–B cut line and its large section appear together. |
| 30–40 s | 「紫のA–B線と下の断面は、同じ場所です。」 | Move/focus across the section once. | The matching position moves along the map cut line. No route or target relationship is inferred. |
| 40–55 s | 「形が見えても、実際の歩きやすさはデータだけでは決まりません。」 | Select `確認場所を見る`. | The section closes, the camera moves to the real PLATEAU road surface, and that road is highlighted. |
| 55–60 s | 「この場所で確かめる都市の状態だけを、4件に絞っています。」 | Point to the checklist; optionally choose the building target. | The target geometry and the associated 3–5 checks change together; status remains `未確認`. |

The default path is three primary clicks. Selecting another candidate adds one click. The presenter should not open Advanced, claim a completed field test, or say that PLATEAU road surfaces prove walkability.
